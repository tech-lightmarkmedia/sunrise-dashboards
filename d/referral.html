Referral Activity — Guide

What this dashboard shows

Every contact who has referred at least one other contact in HubSpot, and the investment capital that flowed from those referrals. One row per referrer. Click any row to see the referral chain and the individual investments behind the number.

Where the referral links come from

HubSpot contact-to-contact associations carrying the custom labels Referral and Referrer. Read from a given contact, an association labelled Referral (typeId 1) points at someone that contact referred; Referrer (typeId 2) points at the person who referred them. The pair is reciprocal, so the graph can be walked from either end.

Unlabelled contact-to-contact associations (HubSpot's generic typeId 449) are ignored — a contact whose only link is unlabelled does not appear here.

Data sources

Referral graph — /cached-referrals, built nightly by the worker. It reads the cached contact base and batch-reads contact associations, keeping only the two labelled types. If the number here looks stale after someone adds associations in HubSpot, the graph refreshes on the next nightly rebuild.

Contacts — /cached-contacts, for names and emails.

Investments — /cached-investments, joined to contacts association-first with an email fallback (the same resolver the Campaign ROI dashboard uses), so an investment whose email_address doesn't match the contact's primary email still counts as long as the records are associated.

Rolled Up vs Direct Only

Rolled Up (default) credits a referrer with capital from their direct referrals and from anyone those people went on to refer, to any depth. If A refers B and B refers C, then C's investments count toward A as well as toward B — so the same capital deliberately appears under more than one referrer, and the column does not sum to a portfolio total.

Direct Only restricts every figure to first-degree referrals. Each investment is then credited to at most one referrer.

The chain walk carries a visited-set guard, so a self-referral or a loop (A refers B, B refers A) is counted once and cannot spin.

Columns

Direct Referrals — contacts this person referred first-hand.

All Referrals — every contact reachable down the chain, excluding the referrer themselves. Equals Direct Referrals in Direct Only mode.

Investors — how many of those referred contacts have at least one investment inside the selected date range.

Capital — total investment amount from those referred contacts in range. The referrer's own investments are never counted here.

Conversion — Investors ÷ All Referrals, to one decimal place.

Date range

The picker filters investments by funds_received — the date money actually arrived, not the record's create date. Referral links themselves are not date-filtered, because HubSpot associations carry no timestamp. So a referral made in 2024 whose investment funded in 2026 shows up in a 2026 range.

Referral counts (Direct, All) are therefore constant across ranges; only Investors, Capital, and Conversion move.

Known gaps

Investments that resolve to no cached contact are counted in the note above the table. They're the same population the Campaign ROI dashboard reports as "not attributed" — investors funding under a secondary email, duplicate contacts, or records not yet linked. Fixed in HubSpot by merging or adding the secondary email, after which the next cache rebuild picks them up with no code change.

Referral relationships tracked anywhere other than these association labels — in a deal property, a note, or a spreadsheet — are invisible here.
