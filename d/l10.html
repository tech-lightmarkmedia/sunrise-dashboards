L10 Dashboard — Guide

Purpose

A quick-glance dashboard for the IR team's L10 meeting. Shows quarterly capital raise performance with a comparison indicator, plus recent activity in a configurable rolling window.

Quarter vs. Quarter

Use the two dropdowns to select any period and comparison from the trailing 12 quarters. Defaults to the current quarter vs. the previous quarter.

Shown as three rows: All Non-Income Funds, Sunrise Income Fund, and Combined. All three read HubSpot Investment records and split on the fund_invested property, so the two fund rows always add to the combined row.

Total Raise: Total funded capital (from HubSpot Investment records, anchored on funds_received date) for the selected period. The delta shows the change vs. the comparison period at the same elapsed point.

New Money: Capital from investments classified as "New" (type_of_investment = New).

Repeat Money: Capital from Repeat and Bolt-On investments.

A note on the Income Fund row: every Income Fund investor is an existing investor who first funded a growth fund, so Income Fund capital is repeat capital by nature. The New/Repeat split still reads type_of_investment exactly as it is written on the record — nothing is assumed — so if Income Fund is opened to new investors later, that money appears in New Money with no change to this dashboard.

The comparison uses same-point-in-quarter logic: if the selected period is the current quarter and today is Day 35, the comparison uses the first 35 days of the comparison quarter. For completed quarters, the full quarter is compared against the same number of days in the comparison quarter.

Recent Activity

Also split into All Non-Income Funds, Sunrise Income Fund, and Combined. The Income Fund row has no Discovery Calls card because that pipeline has no discovery-call stage — Income Fund investors are existing investors and do not go through a discovery call.

Discovery Calls Set: Meetings with call_and_meeting_type = "Discovery Call - Main" or "Discovery Call - Setters" created in the window. Uses meeting creation date (when booked).

Commitments Made: For non-Income funds, deals entering the InvestNext commit pipeline stage in the window. For the Income Fund, deals entering its own Commitment Made stage. Shows count and total dollar amount.

Investments Funded: For non-Income funds, Investment records with funds_received in the window. For the Income Fund, deals entering Closed Won — Income Fund investment records are already reported in the quarter comparison above, so using the deal stage here avoids counting the same money twice on one screen.

Why the two rows measure differently: the non-Income row counts Investment records and the Income Fund row counts deals. The combined row adds them, so treat it as a volume indicator rather than a reconciliation of the two sources.

Fund 5 — Investment Type

Breaks down all Fund 5 investment records (all-time, not date-windowed) by the investment_type property — the legal/account structure the money came through (Individual, Joint, Entity, Trust & Solo 401k, A Self-Directed IRA, Individual (Joint Tenants), An Entity or a Trust).

Unknown means the record has no investment_type value set yet — either it was created after the property was added and never filled in, or it's one of the records flagged during backfill as needing manual review because the linked contact's deals disagreed on which value was correct. Click into Unknown to see which records still need attention.

This section fetches live from HubSpot on every load/refresh rather than reading the nightly cache, since investment_type is a newly added property not yet included in the cache's property list.

Sunrise Income Fund — Pipeline Stage

Income Fund deals live in their own HubSpot pipeline with their own stages — Prospect Engaged, Prospect Contacted, Presentation Scheduled, Commitment Made, Documents Signed, Funds Received, Closed Won. The Main Sales Pipeline's discovery and decision stages do not exist here, which is why the Income Fund never appears in the discovery-call or conversion numbers.

The same three stages are shown two different ways, because they answer different questions:

Current State counts each deal once, at whichever stage it sits on right now. This is a snapshot with no date window — it answers "how much is committed but not yet closed." Because it reads the deal's present stage, a deal appears in exactly one card.

Flow counts deals that entered each stage inside the selected window, read from the stage-entry timestamps. Those timestamps persist after a deal moves on, so one deal can appear in more than one Flow card — that is correct, since it did pass through each stage. This answers "what moved in the last N days." The window selector applies only to this row.

Funded reads Closed Won, not Funds Received. Deals pass through Funds Received on the way to Closed Won, which is where funded deals come to rest. Counting Funds Received in the Current State row would show near-zero, since deals do not stay there. A deal in transit at Funds Received will briefly appear in no Current State card.

Dollar figures come from the deal amount property. Every card is clickable and opens the underlying deals.

Fund vs. Fund — Raise Comparison

Compares Fund 4 and Fund 5 at the same stage of each raise. Pick a date range from the standard picker — it applies directly to Fund 5 (the active raise). Fund 4 shows the same window shifted back exactly 2 years. Since both funds opened Nov 1 (Fund 4: 2023, Fund 5: 2025), the 2-year shift lands on the identical point in Fund 4's raise, so you're always comparing like-for-like age. Default is This Year.

Each fund card shows New money (type_of_investment = "New") and Return money (Repeat + Bolt-On) as dollars and investment counts, a combined Total, and an Investors count — distinct associated contacts (by HubSpot contact association, falling back to funding email where no association exists). Investment amounts are dated by funds_received; per-card dates show each fund's actual window.

The Sunrise Income Fund is intentionally not in this comparison. The 2-year shift exists because Fund 4 and Fund 5 launched two years apart; the Income Fund runs concurrently with Fund 5, so there is no equivalent shift and no like-for-like raise to compare it against.

Data Sources

Investments and meetings from the nightly R2 cache (rebuilt 2 AM ET). Commitments and the Fund 5 investment-type breakdown fetched live from HubSpot. Use ↻ Refresh to reload.
