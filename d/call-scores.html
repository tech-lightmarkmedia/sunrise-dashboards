How this dashboard works

Each Intro Call (Kayla, Cierra) and Discovery Call (Bronson, Kelvin) that followed a booked HubSpot meeting is pulled with its full transcript, then scored by Claude against the Sandler rubric below. Scores are an AI first pass — use them to spot patterns and pick calls to review, not as a final grade.

Which calls are included

  • Meetings typed Intro Call – Setters/Main or Discovery Call – Setters/Main, outcome Completed (or still "Scheduled" after the meeting time).
  • Booked Intro / Discovery: matched to the longest connected call by the meeting owner to the same contact, from 2 hours before to 4 hours after the start (Discovery: 3+ minutes). Meetings marked Disqualified are included.
  • Intro — unscheduled: Kayla or Cierra outbound calls, outcome Connected or Connect – Scheduled, 3+ minutes, not tied to a booked Intro, and classified by Claude as a real intro conversation (callbacks, rescheduling, follow-ups and existing investors excluded).

Sandler steps (0–5 each)

  • Bonding — real rapport; the prospect is comfortable and talking.
  • Up-Front Contract — purpose, time, what each side wants, "no" is OK, and what happens at the end.
  • Pain — questions that go from surface reason to impact and feeling (pain funnel).
  • Budget — investable amount, source of funds, willingness to commit.
  • Decision — who else is involved, how they decide, timeline.
  • Next Step — a dated, agreed next step, or a clean disqualification.
  • Fulfillment (Discovery only) — present only against the pain uncovered.
  • Post-Sell (Discovery only) — lock in the decision and head off back-outs.
  • Talk Control, No Premature Pitch, Sandler Techniques — prospect talks more, no pitching before qualifying, use of reversing / negative reverse selling.

Overall = average of the scored items ÷ 5 × 100.

Account Managers (v1.4)

  • Every connected call by Alex Zahn and Daniel Ackerman that has a transcript. Claude sorts each into Quarterly check-up, SIF pitch, Onboarding or Other/admin (K-1s, paperwork, voicemail, too short). Other/admin calls are listed but not scored.
  • All types: Bonding, Up-Front Contract, Discovery (goals, life events, liquidity, new money), Service (accurate answers, clear expectations), Next Step, Talk Control.
  • Check-up adds: Retention (handled worries or redemption signals) and Opportunity (spotted reinvestment or referral without forcing it).
  • SIF pitch adds: Need/Fit, Budget, Decision, Commitment. Onboarding adds: Clarity of next steps.
  • SIF deal after = a Sunrise Income Fund deal was created for the investor after the call. Same compliance flags as the sales calls, plus a Pressure flag.

Outcome columns

  • Intro rows: Disco booked = a Discovery Call meeting was created for the contact after the call. Disco held = that meeting is marked Completed.
  • Discovery rows: IDC booked = an Investment Decision Call was created after the call. Committed = the deal is at InvestNext Committed or later, or Closed Won in Main Sales, Fund 5 Main Sales or Sunrise Income Fund.
  • Recent calls haven't had time to convert — read the last two weeks with that in mind.

Clicking

  • Click any heatmap score to list that rep's calls lowest-first on that step; click an outcome % to list the calls behind it; click the Weakest step or Compliance tiles to drill in.

Topics

  • The Topic filter finds calls where a subject came up (W-2 income, depreciation, 1031, IRA, etc.). v1.2 tags are keyword matches — good for finding calls, but can include passing mentions. Each call's detail shows the first matching line and time.

Known limits (v1)

  • Calls cut off by a bad connection score low on the end-of-call steps.
  • Discovery Calls that ended in a clean disqualification were scored on Post-Sell instead of marked n/a.
  • Data and compliance "reviewed" marks load from the Worker behind the dashboards login, so reviews are shared. If the Worker can't be reached the page falls back to an offline copy and shows a ⚠ in the header.
